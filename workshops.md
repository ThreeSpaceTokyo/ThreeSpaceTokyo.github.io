## Upcoming

### Art, Science and Complexity
**A fishbowl discussion with Ulrike Kuchner and Mark Neyrinck**  
**Date:** Saturday, October 31, 2026, 18:10–19:30  
**Location:** Three Space Tokyo, Asakusabashi 3-6-6, Taito-ku, Tokyo  
**Admission:** Free. No registration needed — just come along!

How can art and science help us make sense of — and act within — complex worlds? From ecology and AI to the cosmic web, join us to explore what artistic and scientific perspectives can offer each other.

In this participatory “fishbowl” conversation, you can listen, then take a seat to join the discussion. Together, we’ll trace emerging questions and connections through a live visual map. Artists, scientists and anyone curious are welcome. Bring your questions!

[日本語]

**アート・科学・複雑性 — Ulrike KuchnerとMark Neyrinckによる参加型対話**  
2026年10月31日（土）18:10–19:30 ／ Three Space Tokyo（東京都台東区浅草橋3-6-6）／ 参加無料・予約不要

アートと科学は、複雑な世界を理解し、その中で行動するために、どんな手がかりを与えてくれるでしょうか。生態系やAIから宇宙の大規模構造まで。アートと科学の異なる視点を持ち寄り、一緒に考えます。

「フィッシュボウル」は、参加者が席を入れ替わりながら会話に加わる対話形式です。まずは耳を傾け、話したくなったら輪の中へ。生まれた問いやつながりを、その場で視覚的なマップにしていきます。アーティスト、科学者、そして関心のある方ならどなたでも。あなたの問いを持ち寄ってください！

### Mending Workshop Asakusabashi — Kintsugi
<a href="/mending/"><img src="/assets/mending/seam-closeup.jpg" width="320" alt="Gold seam on an antique dish"></a>

**Schedule:** Every Tuesday and the first Thursday of each month, 10:00–12:00 and 14:00–16:00  
**Led by:** Linh Nguyen ([@lynh.ko](https://www.instagram.com/lynh.ko/))  
**Group size:** Max. 4 guests · **Price:** ¥20,000 per person (¥22,000 incl. tax)  
**Languages:** English, Japanese, French, Vietnamese  
**Location:** Three Space Tokyo, Asakusabashi 3-6-6, Taito-ku, Tokyo

A two-hour, hands-on kintsugi workshop using natural urushi and real gold — no epoxy, no substitutes. Each guest finishes the repair of an antique vessel prepared in advance by the teacher: painting a line of urushi along the seam and sprinkling it with gold using the maki-e technique, then takes the piece home. Beginners and visitors to Japan welcome.

### Works in Progress – in situ
**Instagram:** [@wip.in.situ](https://www.instagram.com/wip.in.situ/)  
**In collaboration with:** [@artbytecritique](https://www.instagram.com/artbytecritique/) & [@threespacetokyo](https://www.instagram.com/threespacetokyo/)  
**Location:** Three Space Tokyo, Asakusabashi 3-6-6, Tokyo

The 2026 Works in Progress – in situ call has finished, and our current cohort will present an exhibition in December. The open call for the next Works in Progress – in situ is currently announced through our [exhibition open call](/opencall/) — please submit there, and leave a note if you are only interested in Works in Progress and not an exhibition.

Works in Progress – in situ is an experimental project designed to bring people together to create new networks, new knowledge, and new possibilities. Art Byte Critique and Three Space Tokyo act as facilitators, but it is the sum of our collective energy, creativity, and openness that moves the project forward. It is an opportunity to step into an unmapped creative journey and move your practice forward.

Works in Progress – in situ has a limited capacity of 10 artists / artist groups to facilitate substantive dialogue and communication. If the number of applications exceeds 10, participants will be selected based on their application.

**Guidelines**

1. This project is intended for those based in and around the Kanto area. Participants must be able to attend all meetings in person at Three Space Tokyo from July through November, as well as participate in the Open Incubator Studio at the beginning of December.
2. Participants should be 18 years or older.
3. Participants should have a project or concept in mind that they would like to develop with the support of other participants, and be open to working collaboratively.
4. Works in Progress is open to all genres — visual arts, performing arts, writing, etc.
5. Works in Progress – in situ is conducted in English. Participants should be able to communicate in English. (Communication in Japanese is possible, but the workshop is conducted in English.)
6. There is no funding or accommodation provided for Works in Progress – in situ.


### Love Unbounded 
**Instagram:** [@loveunboundedtokyo](https://www.instagram.com/loveunboundedtokyo/)  
**Schedule:** Every first Saturday, 16:30–18:00  
**Location:** Three Space Tokyo, Asakusabashi 3-6-6, Tokyo  
**Language:** English

Love Unbounded is a 90-min workshop to explore how we form relationships, how we negotiate intimacy, and how love takes shape across different lives. We’ll begin with a brief introduction before moving into a guided open discussion. All identities and relationship styles — monogamous, non-monogamous, or anything beyond labels — are welcome. Please come with curiosity, respect, and presence.

### Three Space To Move
**Instagram:** [@3space2move](https://www.instagram.com/3space2move/)  
**Schedule:** Every first Saturday, 14:00–15:30  
**Led by:** Zhenya ([@negaonegina](https://www.instagram.com/negaonegina/))   
**Location:** Three Space Tokyo, Asakusabashi 3-6-6, Taito-ku, Tokyo

A four-session movement workshop exploring the relationship between body, memory, perception, and space. Through guided movement practices, participants are invited to experience the body as both a physical structure and a sensory environment, moving through layers of awareness, presence, and embodied exploration.
